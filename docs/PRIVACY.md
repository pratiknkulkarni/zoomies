# Privacy Policy — Zoomies

**Last updated:** August 2026

---

## The short version

**Zoomies collects nothing.** It has no accounts, makes no network requests, and
contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code. Everything you log
stays in a database file on your own device.

That is not a policy about how carefully your data is handled elsewhere. There
is no elsewhere.

---

## What is stored, and where

Zoomies keeps your exercises, templates, sessions, sets and settings in a SQLite
database inside the application's private storage on your device. On both
Android and iOS that area is readable only by this application.

Nothing in it is transmitted anywhere. The application works identically in
airplane mode, from installation onwards.

## What is not collected

No name, email address or account of any kind. No advertising identifier, device
identifier or IP address. No usage analytics, session recording or crash
reports. No location, contacts, photos, health data or motion data. No
third-party software development kits that could collect any of these.

## Permissions

Zoomies requests no runtime permissions.

## Sharing your own data

The application can export everything it holds to a JSON file, which you may
then send wherever you choose using your device's own share sheet.

**That transfer is yours, not ours.** The file is written to the application's
cache directory and handed to whichever destination you pick — a messaging app,
a cloud drive, a file manager. Once it leaves, it is governed by that service's
own privacy policy and no longer by this one. Nothing is exported unless you
press the button.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the application removes its database and everything in it. On Android
you may also use **App info → Storage → Clear storage**, which has the same
effect without uninstalling. *Clear cache* does **not** delete your training —
it only removes temporary files such as an export you have already shared.

There is nothing held anywhere else for us to delete, and no request process,
because there is no server holding a copy.

## Children

Zoomies is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone,
including children.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of the application collects anything at all, this policy
will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be
described here rather than only in the date above.
